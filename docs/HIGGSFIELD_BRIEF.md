# Making an AI-generated version in Higgsfield (optional)

`public/praxis-explainer.mp4` is already built from the real UI and diagrams. If you want a cinematic
AI-generated version, use this as the script. Generate each shot as its own 5-8 second clip, then join
them in any editor and add the voiceover.

Tip: upload `public/roadmap.png` and `public/walkthrough.png` as reference images so receipt text and numbers stay exact. AI video models often garble small text, so keep numbers in captions rather than in the generated picture.

## Voiceover (about 75 seconds)
1. "Your boss hands you a shoebox of 24 messy receipts and says: prepare the March expense report."
2. "The result is one spreadsheet, with a Summary of spending, and a list of Flags for the boss."
3. "First, sort every receipt into a bucket: Meals, Travel, Software, Office or Other, and add them up."
4. "One hotel receipt is in euros. The report is in dollars, so 120 euros becomes 132 dollars."
5. "The company has a rule: any single meal over 75 dollars needs a manager's approval, so it goes on the Flags list."
6. "Sometimes the same receipt is saved twice. Same shop, same day, same amount: that is one purchase, so count it once."
7. "Now an AI does this job, and a plain script checks the real file against a secret answer key, out of seven."
8. "Each round you give the AI a little more help: company facts, a new ability, clear instructions. The AI never changes, only the help does."
9. "That is Praxis. Press Watch it happen to try it."

## Shot prompts
1. Top-down, warm desk light: a hand tips a shoebox and crumpled paper receipts spill across a wooden desk. Soft camera push-in. No readable text.
2. A clean laptop screen shows a spreadsheet with two tabs named Summary and Flags. Slow dolly in. Orange accent colour.
3. Colourful paper receipts sliding into five labelled trays. Smooth, playful motion, flat studio background.
4. Close-up of a hotel receipt and a calculator; a euro symbol morphs into a dollar symbol.
5. A restaurant receipt gets a bright orange flag stamped on it. Satisfying stamp motion.
6. Two identical receipts slide together and merge into one. Gentle glow when they merge.
7. A friendly robot arm opens receipt files at a desk, then a green checklist ticks seven boxes.
8. Four steps of a staircase light up one at a time, each step a little brighter.
9. End card on dark background with the word Praxis in orange.
